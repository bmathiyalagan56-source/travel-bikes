Essential Riding Tips

A little preparation helps make every motorcycle ride safer, more comfortable and more enjoyable.

1. Choose the Right Riding Gear

Wear a properly fitted, certified helmet on every ride. A durable jacket, gloves, long pants and over-the-ankle footwear help protect you from weather and road hazards.

2. Inspect Your Bike Before Riding

Take a moment to check the main safety components:

  • Check tire condition and pressure.
  • Test the front and rear brakes.
  • Confirm that the lights and indicators work.
  • Look for loose parts or fluid leaks.

3. Stay Visible and Ride Predictably

Keep a visible position on the road, signal before turning or changing lanes, and leave enough space to react to other road users. Avoid lingering in vehicle blind spots.

4. Adjust for Road and Weather Conditions

Reduce speed on wet, loose or uneven surfaces. Allow extra stopping distance in poor weather, and be especially cautious around intersections and parked cars.

Ride Within Your Limits

Follow traffic laws, avoid distractions, and never ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs. If conditions feel unsafe, stop somewhere secure and reassess.
